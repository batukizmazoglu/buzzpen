Buzzpen

A pocket stylus synthesizer for iPhone and iPad. Slide your finger across 20 metal keys and get a buzzy, vintage square-wave sound. Free, offline, no ads, no account. Optional one-time Pro purchase adds recording, MIDI out and extra colours.
